# IBM Watson Marketing Customer Value Analysis

## Project Overview
This project utilizes the IBM Watson Marketing Customer Value dataset to analyze customer behavior and demographics. The objective is to uncover actionable insights for improving customer retention, optimizing marketing strategies, and enhancing overall customer satisfaction.

## Dataset Details
The dataset includes comprehensive customer information for an auto insurance company:
- **Customer Demographics**: State, Education, Employment Status, Gender, Income, and Marital Status.
- **Policy Details**: Policy Type, Coverage, Number of Policies, and Monthly Premium Auto.
- **Customer Metrics**: Customer Lifetime Value, Total Claim Amount, Months Since Last Claim, and more.
- **Sales Metrics**: Effectiveness of Sales Channels and Renew Offer Types.
- **Vehicle Insights**: Vehicle Class and Vehicle Size.

## Business Objective
- Identify patterns in customer behavior and demographics.
- Improve customer retention through targeted marketing strategies.
- Maximize customer lifetime value and optimize revenue generation.
